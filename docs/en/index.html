What it is

Works offline

The voice lives on your computer or home server. No account, no API key, no monthly fee, no character limit. Your text goes nowhere.

Gets the stress right

Lithuanian has three pitch accents, and standard espeak-ng merges them into one. Ingutė, like Reginutė, takes the stress from a dictionary, not from espeak's guess.

Numbers in the right case

„Susitinkam 15:00“, „nuo 2000 eurų“, „25 °C“ — numbers, times, units and abbreviations are expanded into words with the right Lithuanian endings before synthesis, not read digit by digit.

Two sisters, one toolkit

Ingutė shares the phonemizer, the dictionary and the synthesis wrapper with Reginutė, but speaks in her own voice. The same code serves both — only the model and its config change.

In the Piper catalogue

Since 20 October 2026 Ingutė is in the official Piper catalogue. She should appear in the Home Assistant Piper add-on once Piper 1.9.0 is out with the Lithuanian phonemizer. Until then — through the Wyoming server from the repository, about 5 minutes (README_EN, “Home Assistant”).

News: Lithuanian ears — LT_ausys

A voice assistant has two halves: a mouth and ears. Since 24 October 2026 there are ears that are fast on an ordinary CPU: LT_ausys. It is Kristijonas Jakubsonas's model kmynas (NVIDIA Parakeet, trained on LIEPA-3), converted by us to ONNX and compressed so that it runs without a graphics card. Model on Hugging Face, licence CC BY 4.0. Together with this voice it makes a complete Lithuanian voice assistant for Home Assistant that runs entirely at home.

Lightning fast

Recognises a voice command in 0.21 s. Whisper-family models (Paprika included) take 3.55 s on the same mini PC: they encode a 30-second window for every phrase, however short.

No graphics card

4 CPU cores and 4 GB of RAM are enough — a mini PC or a Proxmox container. The model is 0.9 GB.

More accurate

On read speech (FLEURS) it gets 10.6 % of words wrong, Paprika 12.0 %. Home Assistant's default Whisper base in Lithuanian: 88 %.

At home, not in the cloud

Everything runs on your own machine; nothing is sent anywhere. Home Assistant connects the ears the same Wyoming way as the voice.

A different engine — started differently

Paprika is a Whisper model: it runs on faster-whisper, the same engine as the usual Home Assistant Whisper add-on. LT_ausys is a different kind of model (Parakeet, ONNX), and that add-on does not offer it by itself — our proposal to add it is still waiting for review. So the ears run in their own small Wyoming server (Python environment or Docker, about 5 minutes, recipe) and are added to Home Assistant as a separate Wyoming service. Paprika can stay next to it as a backup.

In our home

Our speaker, Alisa, has been listening with them since 24 October 2026. First day: 13 commands out of 13, each in about 0.3 s, without any extra training on the owner's voice. One home's experience, not a measurement.

More on Lithuanian speech recognition — setting it up in Home Assistant, the numbers and common questions — on the LT_ausys page.

How to start

You need the released piper-tts wheel — no fork, no patches. The voice also needs the stress-aware phonemizer from the same folder: without it Piper would feed the model bare letters and it would not speak Lithuanian.

Which files you need and a short Python example — in the README; for Home Assistant — a tested recipe in “Home Assistant”. Model (.onnx, 63 MB) — Hugging Face or GitHub Release.
